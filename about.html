ABOUT US

PREMIER MANUFACTURER OF CARBON FIBER FABRICS

Formosa Taffeta Co., Ltd. (FTC) was founded in 1973 in Taiwan and is a member of the Formosa Plastic Group. With over 50 years of expertise in textile manufacturing, FTC specializes in carbon fiber fabrics and composite materials that deliver strength, durability, and reliability.

We are committed to innovation and customer satisfaction, providing high-performance solutions for a wide range of industrial applications worldwide.
